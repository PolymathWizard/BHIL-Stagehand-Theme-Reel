About the reel

A screening room for the Stagehand Theme Codex.

The codex is the rulebook. This page is the proof that every rule renders.

  • 20Themes

    Original treatments of film, TV and game genres, each with tokens, open-license fonts, a motion grammar and a prompt seed.

  • 5Families

    Digital Frontier, Retro Signal, Epic Cinema, Worlds and Maps, Auteur and Title.

  • 3Volumes

    Full, Tempered and Boardroom, set per theme from its B2B fit.

  • 18Film passes

    A directed camera, rack focus and foreground parallax, then bloom, halation, light rays, lens dirt, grade, aberration and grain.

What it is

The Stagehand Theme Codex gives page, reel and deck agents twenty motion themes. Each one is a card: tokens, fonts, a motion grammar, a web slot map, a data skin, a Boardroom translation and guardrails.

This page runs every card live, so you can judge a theme before you brief an agent with it.

How to use it

Filter by family or jump to a theme. Turn the dial to see what survives in a boardroom. Click a beat to jump to that slot.

Copy the tokens or the prompt seed into your brief. In the repository, the fit test picks between themes from five essence words and the shape of your data.

How it is built

One self-contained file. Every font is open-license and inlined, and nothing loads from the network.

Every theme charts the same placeholder dataset in its S4 beat: the chart builds, the insight lands, then the data re-sorts and ranks, so data skins compare directly.

Rules it keeps

  • Codenames only: no franchise names, logos, characters or quotes.
  • Three flashes a second at most; ambient flicker at 3% opacity and 2 Hz at most; no full-frame flashes.
  • Theme ink at 11:1 contrast or better, measured.
  • All figures in the data beats are placeholders.

Controls

  • Motion plays on load. Pause motion stops all of it.
  • Boardroom swaps in each theme's B2B translation; themes without one say so on the stage.
  • The 2.39 letterbox previews a theme in a cinema frame.
